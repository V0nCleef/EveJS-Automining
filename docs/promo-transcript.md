# AutoMining 1.2.14 promo transcript

The video uses the current in-game AutoMining menu recorded by the author, cropped for readability. Captions are editorial explanations. Music is an original synthesized score.

## 00:00 — AUTOMINING 1.2.14

Four jobs. One fleet.

- Mining / Hauling / Boosting / PVE
- Pick a job to reveal its settings.
- This is the current in-game menu.

## 00:06 — 01 / MINING

Set up your mining run.

- Mine ore, ice or gas with fitted modules.
- Automatic locks, optional approach and target priorities.
- Set a survey interval and enable accessible compression.

## 00:14 — 01 / MINING FILTER

Choose what you mine.

- Real resource icons. Search and click to build your filter.
- Move resources up or down to set priority.
- Choose nearest, furthest, largest or smallest within a grade.

## 00:26 — 01 / MINING DRONES

Put your drones to work.

- Choose drone groups and mining priorities.
- Focus on one asteroid or spread across several.
- Recall miners for rats, launch fighters, then return to mining.

## 00:36 — 01 / ORE HANDLING

Your ore. Your workflow.

- Keep aboard, jettison, unload and return, request pickup, or use a booster’s fleet hangar.
- Reuse a suitable bin. Optional abandonment opens it to collectors.
- Set a hold trigger. Auto-stack ore hold and fleet hangar.

## 00:48 — 02 / HAULING

Collect. Deliver. Repeat.

- Shared ore pickups across fleets and solar systems. Reserve bins to prevent duplicate pickups.
- Join the requesting fleet temporarily; leave after docking.
- Choose destination/storage, fullness threshold and partial-cargo idle time.

## 01:02 — 03 / BOOSTING

Keep the fleet running.

- Mining bursts and optional mining drones.
- Independent Industrial Core and Asteroid Ore Compressor switches.
- Per-equipment activation intervals, with Reset to equipment default.

## 01:12 — 03 / ORE AND FUEL

An ore hub. A fuel plan.

- Accept compressed ore, raw ore or both from nearby fleet miners.
- Compressed-only reception waits for compression between timed activations.
- Restock Heavy Water from a separate station and stock source. Set reserve and target cycles.

## 01:24 — 04 / PVE ASSIGNMENTS

Protect your mining fleet.

- Belt patrol: select a belt and engage its rats.
- Fleet escort: follow your selected pilot; defend the grid.
- Reinforcement standby: respond to calls within your jump limit, then return home.

## 01:34 — 04 / COMBAT

Control the fight.

- Focus or spread fire. Strongest or weakest enemy first.
- Native weapon groups are honoured. Automatic orbit uses your highest-DPS weapon’s range.
- Clear the rats, then orbit a fleetmate. Empty grid? Stop and wait.

## 01:44 — 04 / AMMO AND DRONES

Ready for the next fight.

- Save a separate combat-drone profile for PVE.
- Compatible ammo appears for your fitted weapons. Set extra cargo-round targets.
- Restock when docked from accessible stock. No ammo or fuel is bought.

## 01:52 — SHARED / FLEET

Keep the team together.

- Fleet invites, saved roster presets and nearby automatic grouping.
- Call reinforcements manually or when attacked. Set a responder limit.
- The fleet boss can stop AutoMining across the fleet and order drones home.

## 02:00 — SHARED / DEFENSE

Plan your way out.

- Shield and armor retreat thresholds for every job.
- Choose a station or structure and accessible storage.
- Travel recall uses native drone rules. Urgent retreat can leave drones behind.

## 02:08 — SHARED / STATISTICS

Every run has a story.

- Pilot and fleet views: mined ore, pickups, deliveries, rat kills, damage and docks.
- Start a new run to archive and reset your own counters. Browse Sessions.
- Settings stay saved per character and job. Nine interface languages.

## 02:16 — AUTOMINING 1.2.14

Choose a job. Build your fleet.

- Available for EveJS 0.12.9.
- Open Mods → AutoMining or type !automining.
- Download and full settings guide on GitHub.
